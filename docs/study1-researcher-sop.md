# Study 1 Researcher SOP

## Before a Session

- Confirm the active release manifest and checksum.
- Confirm all three participant links are assigned to P, T1, and T2.
- Confirm consent, microphone preflight, ASR, LLM, TTS, recorder, and LiveKit
  health in the researcher console.
- Confirm the task materials are the approved Hidden Profile version.

## During a Session

- Advance phases only from the researcher console.
- Record a reason for every pause, resume, extension, termination, forced
  advance, Summary retry, transcript correction, or incident marker.
- Do not expose P to the delegated Proxy meeting while P is isolated.
- During handoff, wait for the system to show P joined, X stopped, and T1/T2
  still connected before moving into the synchronous meeting.

## After a Session

- Review Summary QA, transcript corrections, typed markers, replay windows, and
  integrity warnings.
- Export the complete ZIP and store it according to the privacy runbook.
- Record any known issue that could affect interpretation of the data.

## Stop Conditions

Stop or terminate the session if role isolation fails, the wrong participant can
access private material, the Proxy reads unauthorized material, or the release
checksum does not match between A and B.
